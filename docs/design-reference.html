Every city has places the map doesn't know. This is the visual source of truth for the app: match these nine screens, these tokens and these typefaces, in every Indian script.

The field notebook and the painted signboard

An explorer's notebook — paper, dashed trails, ink stamps, handwritten tips — meets the hand-painted signboards and railway posters of Indian towns: bold display type, flat layered landscapes, laterite and marigold.

Photos and videos are the hero; Watch is the one dark, immersive surface. The dashed footpath is the signature: progress in Add, last-mile routes, the edge of cleared fog, Partner progress, and the logo.

Nine screens

Phone at 360 × 780 (build at 390 × 844; same proportions). Illustrations stand in for real photos. Use the theme switch above to check dark mode.

Sample content: place and city names are real, but all counts, users, earnings, tips, live reports, last-mile directions and safety notes in these mock-ups are invented for illustration. Illustrations are placeholders for user photos; in the app, places without photos show the category illustration (SPEC §12.6).
